About Us

A Colombo dining story, built one branch at a time

Mr. Deen began as a single restaurant in Wellawatte and has grown into one of Colombo's most recognised casual dining ecosystems-known across the city for massive portion sizes, Instagram-worthy presentation, fusion fast food, and late-night accessibility.

Our philosophy

Every Mr. Deen concept is built around the same core belief: a meal should be generous, memorable, and worth photographing before it's worth eating. That shows up differently at each location-full-course family spreads in Wellawatte, gourmet fast-casual builds at RoadHouse, and an unhurried cup of chai at the Kade-but the standard never moves.

We fuse Indian, Western, and Sri Lankan street-food traditions into dishes that feel both familiar and genuinely new, and we season the room the same way we season the food: bold, warm, and a little theatrical.

Leadership

Meet the Founder

Ishrath Deen

Founder, Director & CEO

Ishrath Deen founded Mr. Deen with a simple ambition: build a Colombo restaurant brand that people would talk about before they even sat down. What started as a single flagship in Wellawatte has, under his direction, grown into a three-concept ecosystem spanning full-course dining, fast-casual street food, and a dedicated chai counter-each one carrying the same obsession with scale, flavour, and presentation.

As Director and CEO, Ishrath continues to oversee the brand's growth, menu direction, and the standard that every new Mr. Deen location is held to: generous portions, fusion flavour, and a room worth posting about.

"Eat good, feel good isn't a slogan here-it's the brief for every dish that leaves our kitchen."
How We Got Here

The Mr. Deen timeline

The Beginning

Mr. Deen Wellawatte opens

The flagship launches on Station Road with a menu built around full-course Indian curries, biriyani, and hearty burgers-and quickly becomes known for portion sizes that need to be shared.

Growth

A second concept: RoadHouse

Recognising a different kind of crowd along Marine Drive, the brand launches RoadHouse Kollupitiya-a faster, trendier counterpart built on smash burgers and loaded fries.

Expansion

Chai Kade joins the family

Co-located with RoadHouse, Chai Kade gives the ecosystem its slow, roadside counterpart-spiced chai and light bites for guests who want to linger a little longer.

Today

3,600+ reviews and counting

With a combined rating built on thousands of guest reviews, Mr. Deen is now one of Colombo's most talked-about dining ecosystems-online and in person.
